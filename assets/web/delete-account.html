Delete your account

You can permanently delete your iClose account and its associated data at any time — directly in the app, or by contacting us. Here's exactly how it works and what happens to your data.

Delete from within the app

  1. Open the iClose app and sign in.
  2. Go to Account (bottom tab).
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm. Your account and personal data are permanently removed.

Prefer to email us?

Send a deletion request from your registered email address to hello@iclose.ae with the subject “Delete my account.” We verify ownership and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your name, email address and phone number
  • Your profile and account settings
  • Saved bank accounts and IBAN details
  • Uploaded documents and verification files
  • Your inquiries, deals and in-app activity
  • Push notification tokens

What we may retain

To comply with UAE anti-money-laundering, tax and real-estate regulations, we may retain a limited record of completed financial transactions (e.g. deal and payout records) for the period required by law, after which it is permanently deleted. This retained data is not used to identify or contact you for any other purpose.

  • Completed transaction / payout records — retained only as required by applicable law, then deleted

Questions

Contact us any time at hello@iclose.ae. See also our Privacy Policy.
